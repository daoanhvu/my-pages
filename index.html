Privacy Policy

iProtectThem for iOS
Effective date: 1 November 2026

This policy describes how the iOS app iProtectThem handles information. The app is developed by Vu Dao (“we”, “us”). iProtectThem is a personal vault. You create an app login password on your iPhone, then store items such as notes, passwords, addresses, and medication details on that device.

Information stored on your device

The information below stays in the app on your iPhone. We do not operate an account server, and the app does not send this information to us.

  • Vault items you create, including titles, categories, contents, password-change dates, and medication details.
  • Photos you take with the camera, and files you choose to attach to an item.
  • A hash of your app login password, used only to unlock the app on that device. We do not receive the password.
  • Your choice to allow Face ID or Touch ID, and whether the Premium unlock is active on that device.

Face ID and Touch ID are handled by iOS. The app receives only success or failure. It does not receive or store your biometric data.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect, sell, or share personal information. In particular, the app does not include:

  • An account you register with us
  • Analytics, advertising, or tracking
  • A connection that uploads your vault to us

Permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use the related feature. You can refuse, and you can turn a permission off later in iOS Settings.

  • Camera. Used only when you choose to photograph an attachment for an item. The photo is stored with that item on your device.
  • Face ID. Used only if you turn on biometric unlock in the app.
  • Notifications. Used only for medication reminders you set up. A reminder can show the item title, dosage, and note on your device. iOS delivers that reminder locally. It is not sent to us. What appears on the Lock Screen follows your iOS notification settings.
  • Files. When you import data or attach a file, iOS lets you pick that file. The app reads only the file you select, and stores it on your device.

Import and export

If you import a file, the app reads it to load items onto your device. If you export, the app writes a file to the location you choose, such as Files or another app you pick. Those files are not uploaded to us.

Purchases

Premium is an optional one-time purchase through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your card number or billing address. The app stores only whether Premium is unlocked on that device. Apple’s privacy policy describes how Apple handles App Store purchases: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Backups and deletion

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, that backup can include this app’s data. Those backups are controlled by your device settings and by Apple, not by us.

There is no iProtectThem account to delete. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes the vault stored by the app on that device. Reminders scheduled by the app are removed with it. A copy can remain in a device backup until you delete that backup.

Children

iProtectThem is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes
